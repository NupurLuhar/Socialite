# Socialite

Socialite - A Social Media Platform
Socialite is a modern, engaging, and user-friendly social media platform designed to connect people from all walks of life. It allows individuals to share their thoughts, moments, experiences, and create a community around their interests. Whether you're sharing a photo, making a new connection, or engaging with global trends, Socialite creates a space where everyone can belong, express themselves, and be heard.

Core Features:
User Profiles:

Users can create and personalize their profiles with photos, bio, and personal information.
Profiles also display the user's activity, such as posts, likes, comments, and shared media.
Post Sharing:

Users can share a variety of media types such as text, images, videos, and links.
Posts can be publicly shared or restricted to a certain group of friends or followers.
Social Feed:

A continuous stream of updates from friends, followed pages, and groups.
Posts are shown in chronological order, but users can filter the feed by preferences or topics of interest.
Friendship & Following:

Users can send friendship requests, follow others, or join public groups.
The platform allows for both private and public interactions depending on user preferences.
Real-time Chat & Messaging:

Private messaging and real-time chat features to connect with friends, family, or like-minded individuals.
Group chats allow users to collaborate or share ideas with multiple users at once.
Notifications:

Real-time notifications for comments, likes, mentions, friend requests, and messages, so users stay up to date with interactions.
Likes, Comments, and Shares:

Users can like, comment on, and share posts from others, making the platform interactive and engaging.
Trending Topics & Hashtags:

A dynamic feature that shows trending topics, hashtags, and the latest buzz on the platform, helping users discover the most popular content.
Content Discovery:

A search feature that allows users to discover posts, people, pages, or groups that align with their interests.
Curated recommendations based on user activity, likes, and preferences.
Privacy & Security:

Robust privacy controls to manage who can see your posts, send you messages, and follow you.
Two-factor authentication and advanced security features to ensure user data is protected.
Technologies:
Frontend: React.js 
Backend: Node.js 
Database: MySQL (for storing user data, posts, and interactions)
Styling: CSS
